WEBSITE NARRATIVE — Mettle
This is the story your website tells, not page copy.

The Core Idea (North Star)
Mettle exists to help founders handle complexity with confidence, clarity, and competence.

Everything on the site must reinforce one feeling:

“These people understand messy problems and can turn them into workable systems.”

The Narrative Arc (How a Visitor Experiences the Site)
Act 1 — Recognition (Hero & Above-the-Fold)
Visitor thought:

“This feels like it’s written for my problem, not selling a service.”

You immediately communicate:

We work with complex, ambiguous problems

We are consulting-led, not tool-led

We partner with founders, not just deliver tasks

Key emotion: Relief
Key signal: Maturity

Act 2 — Credibility (What You Actually Do)
Visitor thought:

“They’ve solved problems like mine — even if not in my exact industry.”

Here you:

Show patterns of work, not services

Talk about thinking, systems, outcomes

Demonstrate range without being scattered

Key emotion: Trust
Key signal: Depth over breadth

Act 3 — Differentiation (Why You’re Different)
Visitor thought:

“They don’t jump to solutions — they understand first.”

You emphasize:

Diagnosis before execution

Business understanding before technology

Long-term outcomes over short-term delivery

Key emotion: Confidence
Key signal: Consulting DNA

Act 4 — Proof (Case Studies / Work)
Visitor thought:

“They’ve handled complexity across domains — I can see myself here.”

You:

Tell stories, not feature lists

Show how thinking → decisions → systems → execution

Avoid bragging, focus on outcomes

Key emotion: Belief
Key signal: Experience

Act 5 — Invitation (CTA)
Visitor thought:

“I want to talk to these people.”

Not “Contact Us”.
Not “Get a Quote”.

Instead:

“Start a conversation”

“Discuss your problem”

“Let’s explore the problem together”

Key emotion: Safety
Key signal: Partnership

What the Narrative Explicitly Avoids
❌ “We offer X services”

❌ “We use Y technologies”

❌ Over-claiming scale

❌ Buzzwords

❌ Loud marketing language
